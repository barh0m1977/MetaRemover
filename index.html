Privacy Policy for MetaRemover

Effective Date: July 1, 2026

MetaRemover the app is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your data is handled.

1. Overview

MetaRemover is an offline image processing application designed to remove EXIF and metadata from images. Your privacy is a core principle of this app.

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

2. Data Collection

MetaRemover does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Images or media files
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics
  • Crash reports (unless explicitly added in future updates with user consent)

All image processing happens locally on your device.

3. Image Processing

  • Images selected in the app are processed only on your device
  • No images are uploaded to any server
  • No data is transmitted outside your device
  • All metadata removal happens locally using built-in processing logic

4. Internet Usage

MetaRemover does not require an internet connection to function.

The app does not:

  • Send data to external servers
  • Use cloud storage
  • Integrate third-party tracking services

5. Third-Party Services

MetaRemover does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

6. Data Storage

  • The app does not permanently store your images or metadata
  • Any temporary processing data is cleared immediately after processing

7. Children’s Privacy

MetaRemover does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, the app is safe for all age groups.

8. Security

We prioritize local processing to ensure your data never leaves your device, reducing any risk of exposure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in future versions of the app. Any changes will be reflected in this document with an updated effective date.
